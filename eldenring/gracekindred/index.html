    Grace Kindred | ELDEN RING Accessibility Mod for Blind Players

    Grace Kindred. You'll understand the name of the mod when I release the open-world version.

    Important Notes

    This is a combat-focused public test. If you want to explore and experience ELDEN RING normally, please wait for the open-world build, which is planned for December.

    Since this test focuses on combat and requires the Boss Arena mod, you may encounter spoilers through boss dialogue. You can skip the dialogue or choose to fight minor bosses instead. I also recommend avoiding item descriptions in shops if you want to avoid as many spoilers as possible.

    I will not be responsible if you choose to play vanilla ELDEN RING and get stuck somewhere in the open world. The navigation system is still under development.

    Also, the game's original difficulty is preserved 100%. Nothing has been made easier. This game is not for everyone because it is very difficult. But if you enjoy a challenge, feel free to give it a try.

    About ELDEN RING

    ELDEN RING is an open-world action RPG developed by FromSoftware. You play as a Tarnished, traveling through the Lands Between, exploring dungeons, discovering secrets, fighting enemies, and building your character with a wide range of weapons, spells, equipment, and abilities.

    Combat is challenging and requires patience, timing, positioning, stamina management, and learning enemy attack patterns. Enemies and bosses have different movesets, attack speeds, and openings. Expect to die many times as you learn how to fight them.

    Download, Installation, and Getting Started

    Download the mod and listen demonstration here: Grace Kindred New audio demonstrations are coming soon.

    After downloading Grace Kindred, extract the archive anywhere you like.

    I recommend using the following directory:

    ELDEN RING\Game\me3

    You will need to create the me3 folder yourself. You can also name it mod or anything else. The folder name does not matter.

    Place all files and folders from Grace Kindred inside the me3 folder.

    Next, download the Boss Arena Mod.

    Choose between two versions:

    • Sandbox: All items are unlocked, and upgrades are free.
    • Progression: You must defeat bosses to level up and obtain the items and materials needed to upgrade your character.

    After downloading Boss Arena, extract the archive and place the extracted Boss Arena folder containing the mod folder inside the me3 folder you created earlier.

    For example:

    ELDEN RING\Game\me3\Boss Arena Sandbox\mod

    Once everything is in place, run Launcher.cmd.

    The launcher will ask you to enter the path to the game executable:

    ELDEN RING\Game\eldenring.exe

    Navigate to eldenring.exe, press the Applications key, select Copy as path, and paste the path into the launcher.

    You can then choose to launch vanilla ELDEN RING or the version of Boss Arena you installed.

    Current Features

    • Menu narration: Approximately 90% of the game's menus are accessible. This includes character creation (descriptions are not yet supported), settings, leveling up, character upgrades, weapon upgrades, shops, equipment management, inventory management, Sites of Grace, spells, Ashes of War, and Great Runes.

    • Combat: Spatial 3D audio that follows enemy attack movements. A generic attack warning is also available as an alternative.

    • Navigation: Basic navigation features include wall sounds based on the actual dimensions of obstacles, open-space detection, object tracking, and a scanner with separate categories for items, NPCs, enemies, destructible obstacles, and more.

    • Verbal feedback: Optional spoken feedback for navigation, obstacles, enemy tracking, and other information.

    • Audio customization: Five spatial 3D combat sound presets, three wall sound presets, and customization options for several other sounds. Individual sound volumes and verbal feedback settings can also be adjusted independently.

    • Control customization: Customizable controls for both controllers and keyboards. This feature has received limited testing so far.

    • Boss Arena: The Boss Arena gameplay loop is fully supported.
